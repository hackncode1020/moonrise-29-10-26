# 🌙 Moonrise Tracker: Karwa Chauth Special

A full Karwa Chauth love website for your wife or husband, made with plain **HTML, CSS and JavaScript** (canvas + WebAudio). No libraries and no image files.

**▶ Live:** https://moonrise-hackncode.sites.tab.bot/
**💌 Personal link:** `https://moonrise-hackncode.sites.tab.bot/?name=YourLove`

## ✨ Features
- 🌸 **Mehendi intro**: a golden mehendi mandala draws itself with floating gold particles, then "Karwa Chauth Special 🌙"
- ⏳ **Real moonrise countdown**: pick your city (Ahmedabad, Surat, Vadodara, Rajkot, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi, Jaipur, Chandigarh, Amritsar, Lucknow, Indore, Bhopal, Patna, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Chennai) and see a live "Moonrise in HH:MM:SS" for 29 Oct 2026. Times are approximate, worked out from astronomical moonrise for each city.
- ⚡ **Demo moonrise**: type a name, press **Start 🌙**, and the moon rises in 5 seconds
- 🌕 **Chhalni reveal**: a golden chhalni sweeps over the moon, the moon turns into a heart, a couple appears under the moon, with fireworks, sky lanterns and falling rose petals
- ✍️ Typed text: "Chaand nikal aaya 🌙" → "Tum mera chaand ho, <name> ❤️" → "HAPPY KARWA CHAUTH"
- 💧 **Fasting progress ring** (sunrise → moonrise) with fun stats: Water 0 · Patience ∞ · Love 100% · Hunger 😅
- 🪔 **Animated sargi thali**: a rotating thali with a flickering diya, karwa pot, laddoos, kaju katli, marigolds, sindoor and coconut
- 💫 **Shayari carousel**: 6 romantic Hinglish shayari that auto-swipe and can be swiped by hand
- 💞 **Promise wall**: tap any of the 6 cards to reveal a love promise, or reveal them all
- 📷 **Photo in the moon**: add their photo and it shows inside the moon. It stays on the phone (FileReader), nothing is uploaded.
- 💌 **Wish card**: a canvas card with their name, downloadable as a PNG, plus a WhatsApp share button
- 🎵 Music on/off: a synthesized tanpura drone, bansuri melody and soft tabla (WebAudio)
- 🔗 `?name=` personal links, Share button, Replay
- 📱 Mobile-first, tested at 390×844 and 360×640

## 🛠 Run it
Open `index.html` in a browser. That's it.

## Files
- `index.html`: the page sections
- `style.css`: the festive glass UI
- `script.js`: canvas drawing (mehendi, sky, moon → heart, chhalni, couple, fireworks, thali, wish card), the countdown maths and the music
